What We Do

Full-service sourcing, from first idea to your warehouse

Whether you need one service or the whole chain handled, we plug in exactly where you need us.

Product Sourcing

Tell us what you want to buy or build. We identify vetted manufacturers in China that match your specs, quality bar and budget, then send you real quotes to compare. This is where most projects start, and where the right factory saves you the most money.

Supplier Vetting & Negotiation

We verify business licenses, audit factories and negotiate pricing and payment terms in the supplier's own language. No middlemen markups, no surprises.

Manufacturing Management

From samples and tooling to full production runs, we manage timelines, follow up with the factory and keep your project moving so deadlines actually get met.

Quality Inspection

Pre-production, during-production and pre-shipment inspections at the factory. Defects get caught in China, not in your Canadian warehouse when it's too late.

Shipping & Freight

Sea, air or express. We book the freight, prepare the paperwork and track your cargo from the factory floor to the port of entry.

Customs & Import into Canada

Duties, tariff classification, customs clearance and final delivery. We make sure your goods enter Canada compliantly and land where you need them.
